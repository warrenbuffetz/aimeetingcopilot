Senior Product Manager — Restaurant Product (Toronto, hybrid)

OpenTable's Restaurant Product team builds tools that help restaurants run smarter, more profitable businesses. This role identifies opportunities and drives execution of AI/ML-powered products that directly optimize revenue for restaurants on the platform.

Responsibilities
• Drive product vision and execution for solutions that optimize restaurant revenue.
• Lead end-to-end development of 0-to-1, AI/ML-powered products, from concept through launch.
• Partner with engineering and design to rapidly prototype, test, and iterate on new concepts.
• Use data analytics to identify revenue opportunities, prioritize the roadmap, and measure impact.
• Align cross-functional stakeholders and executives around priorities and trade-offs.
• Translate business objectives into a compelling product narrative.

Minimum qualifications
• 6+ years of product management experience on complex, high-impact initiatives.
• Demonstrated experience building and shipping AI/ML-powered products.
• Proven 0-to-1 product launches.
• Strong business acumen; translate data analytics into strategic product decisions.
• Excellent stakeholder management with executives and cross-functional teams.

Preferred qualifications
• Restaurant, hospitality, or marketplace industry experience.
• Revenue management, pricing, or yield-optimization products.
• Rapid experimentation and agile/lean methods.
• Two-sided marketplace experience.
• Advanced technical fluency in AI/ML concepts and applications.

Compensation (published): CAD $135,000–$165,000 base, plus bonus and equity eligibility (Toronto posting).
