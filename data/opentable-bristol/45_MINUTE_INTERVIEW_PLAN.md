## Speakable answer rule

Lead with a clear recommendation or personal conclusion in the first 10 seconds. Explain the main reason, use one vivid example, name the downside or constraint, and end with how you'd validate or change the decision. Don't narrate the entire six-step framework aloud; use it mentally. Target roughly 45–75 seconds for strategic answers and up to ~2 minutes for end-to-end experience deep dives, then let Bristol probe.

## Your strongest illustrative 0-to-1 idea

**Service-aware revenue advisor (B17):** Predict which *existing* availability or pacing action makes sense given reservation, turn-time and operator constraints. The host approves; the system explains why and falls back safely. Compare it against OpenTable's **existing** rules and controls. The outcome is reliable, incremental seated value and less operational friction — not model accuracy alone.

**Real floor example (SC11):** Two servers call out. The tables still exist, but usable service capacity has changed. Protect confirmed reservations, recommend future pacing changes, don't silently cancel guests, and give the host/manager one-tap control. If the owner resists, explain trade-offs rather than overriding them.

**Global rollout example (SC12/13):** A restaurant in a market with entrenched local booking habits needs more than language localization. Learn the floor and reservation workflow, configure the common platform, validate essential integrations and first live service, then expand by readiness. Roll back the AI layer when quality is inadequate.

## The four questions to keep in reserve for Bristol

1. Suzanne gave me some context on front-of-house and availability planning, while the job description focuses on AI-driven revenue products. How do those priorities come together for this role?
2. Where do you think restaurants are losing the most value that OpenTable is especially well positioned to address?
3. As the platform moves into more markets, where is the greater challenge — local operating differences or scaling the technical platform and integrations?
4. If the person joining does a really strong job in the first six months, what would you hope they'd figured out or delivered?

## Never over-claim

- You haven't shipped an enterprise production AI/ML product as PM. Discuss Masters in Analytics and hands-on prototypes separately (B06).
- Arc'teryx membership's funded phase is not synonymous with a fully shipped membership platform (B43).
- RBC's enterprise service's 5M+ annual settlement events are not volume generated by the cashback withdrawal feature (B40).
- Hypothetical restaurant AI models and outcomes must not be described as your actual achievements.
- The recruiter said not to use AI tools **during the behavioral interview**. Prepare and rehearse beforehand.
